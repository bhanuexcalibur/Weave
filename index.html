02 · VERTICAL DEEP DIVE: DENTAL

The Dental Buying Committee and Clinical Bottlenecks

Understanding the operational dynamics of solo dental practices and emerging Dental Support Organizations (DSOs).

ECONOMIC BUYER

Lead Dentist / Practice Owner

Core Motivation: Maximizing production per chair hour and keeping operatories fully booked with high-margin restorative treatments (crowns, implants, bridge deliveries).

Primary Frustration: High marketing spend wasted when prospective restorative patients bounce to neighboring clinics after reaching voicemail.

Focus Metric: Daily Operatory Production Yield
OPERATIONAL CHAMPION

Office Manager

Core Motivation: Maintaining front-desk sanity during morning check-ins, avoiding double data entry, and rapidly filling last-minute cancellations.

Primary Frustration: Playing phone tag with patients who refuse to answer unknown phone numbers or check voicemail.

Focus Metric: Daily Chair Fill Rate & Recall Recapture
FINANCIAL GATEKEEPER

Treatment & Financial Coordinator

Core Motivation: Securing out-of-pocket patient co-pays and financing approvals before the patient walks out of the consultation room.

Primary Frustration: Patients hesitating on treatment plans due to unexpected upfront costs or awkward paper credit applications.

Focus Metric: CareCredit Case Acceptance Rate
Clinical Bottleneck 01

Front-Desk Morning Influx (Illustrative Scenario)

During peak morning check-ins and simultaneous checkout queues, front-desk staff face trade-offs between attending to physical patients and answering inbound phone calls.

Source: Weave reports that only 20% of people leave voicemails. This is a general platform-reported statistic, not a dental-specific abandonment rate. Weave Missed Call Text
Clinical Bottleneck 02

Unscheduled Treatment Plans

Diagnosed restorative treatment remains unscheduled in the practice-management system when staff lack the capacity for consistent follow-up, and outbound calls frequently reach voicemail.

Front-Desk Workflow Dynamic: Manual phone tag bottlenecks patient recall
Clinical Bottleneck 03

Case Acceptance Hesitation

Patients delay restorative treatments when presented with out-of-pocket costs at checkout, creating an operational opportunity for staff to share financing application links via two-way SMS.

Context: Weave DeSoto Case Study ($31k+ in overall 8-week practice financing; text link attribution not isolated)

04 · STRATEGIC HYPOTHESIS & EXPERIMENT

The Positioning Wedge and 60-Day Mid-Market Growth Pilot

Translating practice insights into a disciplined, testable go-to-market campaign with defined baselines and decision thresholds.

POSITIONING HYPOTHESIS (TO BE TESTED)

"Dentists Don't Buy Phone Software: They Buy Chair-Fill Insurance"

Proposed 60-Day Commercial Pilot

01 · Target Cohort & Baseline Governance

Audience: Solo and 2-location dental practices utilizing supported Dentrix editions with active Henry Schein One API Exchange integration in co-marketing territories (confirming specific edition capabilities, API permissions, and writeback scope during cohort scoping).

Governance: Establish control baselines from historical CRM data, randomize eligible accounts into control and variant messaging, and agree on primary and guardrail metrics with RevOps before launch.

02 · Control vs. Variant Framing

Control Messaging: Standard feature pitch ("Unified VoIP phone system and two-way texting for dental offices").

Variant Messaging: Chair-Fill Insurance pitch (orienting discovery around missed phone opportunities, unscheduled Dentrix treatment recovery, and CareCredit financing).

03 · Funnel Metrics & Precise Definitions

Qualified-Demo Hold Rate: Held qualified demos ÷ scheduled qualified demos. (Hypothetical planning assumption: baseline hold rate to be established from historical Weave CRM data, with target calibrated alongside RevOps to evaluate whether the Chair-Fill Insurance narrative produces a statistically meaningful lift; 58% baseline vs. 70% target shown strictly as an illustrative planning model, not observed company performance).

Stage 2+ Opportunity Creation: Rate of eligible accounts progressing to qualified pipeline stage.

Messaging Adoption: Percentage of eligible reps using variant discovery talk tracks consistently.

04 · RevOps Decision Thresholds & Scale

Statistical Calibration: RevOps will establish baseline rates, sample sizes powered for the agreed minimum detectable effect, observation windows, and decision thresholds from historical CRM data prior to launch.

Provisional Gate: If variant demonstrates significant early-funnel lift and rep adoption over 60 days, advance playbook while tracking downstream win rate as deals mature.
